Users

Commission is a per-rep amount baked silently into the quoted $/KG price — Stretch Film and Strap each have their own commission, independently in either Percent (multiplicative on the price) or Cents/KG (a flat $/KG add-on) mode (e.g. +3¢/KG on Stretch but +5% on Strap for the same rep, at the same time). Works the same for local or foreign reps. Never shown anywhere the customer or the rep can see it (screen, PDF or Excel) — it's for your own reference only, to work out what's owed.

Delete (✕) only works for a user with no saved quotations yet (each quotation records who made it, so deleting would break that history). For anyone who has already quoted, uncheck Active instead — it stops them from signing in without losing their past quotations. You can't delete the account you're currently signed in as.
