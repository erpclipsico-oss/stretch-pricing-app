Pre-Stretch — micron → source SKU mapping

Pre-Stretch (hand-stretch film) is made-to-order: the rep types in roll weight, core weight, rolls/pallet and packaging type per quotation line. Its material cost is not built from the BOM on its own — each Pre-Stretch micron borrows its source jumbo SKU's current finished, margin- inclusive sales price ($/KG) and multiplies it by the line's entered net weight (Stretch!T79 = AI49*J79). Change which jumbo SKU a Pre-Stretch micron borrows from here. See Global Settings for the Dollar Rate – Prestretch and Material Rates for the Core – prestretch and Box rates used in the rest of the formula.
