What "listens" to the final price — quick reference
Verified line by line against the reference Excel sheet (each item below reproduces the sheet's own behaviour exactly, including the ones that don't affect price):
  • ✅ Listens — Material Rates (every resin/packaging rate, incl. Exceed/Vista/Enable/LD/UVI), Electricity tariff, Fixed Costs items, Variable Costs, Margin Factors ("Factors" table), Pallet/Packaging components, BOM recipes, the exchange rate/assumptions below, and the Discount % (capped per-category at the 🔵 "Max Discount" rows below — Standard/Power/Power Plus/Rigid/Prestretch each have their own).
  • ❌ Does NOT listen — the Direct Labor roster page. Its wage/overtime totals are not wired into Fixed Costs; they're purely informational, same as in the reference Excel workbook (its "Direct Labor" roster tab and its "Fixed" cost tab are two separately-maintained sheets there too — not formula-linked). To change what production labor actually costs in a price, edit the "Direct Labor wages" / "Direct Labor overtime" lines directly on the Fixed Costs page.
  • ℹ️ Business-layer extras (not in the Excel sheet at all) — the Color/Foreign-seller/Credit-term extras below are this app's own additive settings; there's nothing to "match against Excel" for them, they always apply as flat surcharges when their checkbox/flag is on.
  • PET/PP Strap — has its own separate page, PET/PP Strap Costing, with the same kind of reference note at the top. It has its own Dollar Rate and its own two Max Discount caps (PET / PP) there — fully independent of every Stretch Film category's own cap below; the Freight table is the one thing still shared by both product lines.
